Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. MobLife uses anonymous authentication automatically on first launch. You don't need an email, password, or any sign-up. Your data is associated with a random anonymous ID stored on your device.

Will I lose my progress if I uninstall and reinstall?

As long as you haven't done a factory reset inside the app, your data is synced to the cloud via Firebase. When you reinstall, the app will restore your progress from the cloud on the first launch — as long as you're on the same device (same anonymous Firebase UID). Switching to a new device will start fresh.

I completed a habit but didn't get an egg / XP. What happened?

Eggs are only awarded for completing habits you haven't already logged today. Each habit can only be completed once per day. If you logged it earlier in the day and completed it again, the second completion won't award additional XP. Check the habit card — a checkmark means it's already logged for today.

How do I get rare or shiny mob creature?

Rarity is determined when an egg hatches. Higher-rarity eggs (rewarded from quests and boss battles) have better odds. Shiny mob creature have a very low chance on any hatch. There's no way to guarantee a shiny — it's intentionally rare.

What are boss battles?

Mini-bosses and weekly bosses appear as special encounters. Mini-bosses appear after strong habit completion streaks. Weekly bosses reset every Monday and offer the largest XP and egg rewards in the game. Check the Quests tab to see if a boss is active.

How do I add custom habits?

Open the Log tab and tap Edit habits, then Add a habit. You can define a name, type, and target. Custom habits earn the same XP as built-in habits.

How do I reset everything and start over?

Open the System tab and tap Factory Reset. This permanently deletes all local data and your cloud data. This action cannot be undone. Your mob creature, XP, and habit history will be gone.

Is the app free? Are there ads or in-app purchases?

MobLife is completely free. There are no ads and no in-app purchases of any kind.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All core features work offline. Your data is saved locally and synced to the cloud when you reconnect.

Something is broken / I found a bug.

Please email mobflow.dev@gmail.com with a description of what happened, what device you're using, and what you expected vs. what occurred. Screenshots are helpful.
