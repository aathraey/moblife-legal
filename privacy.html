5. Data Retention

Your data is retained in Firebase Firestore for as long as you use the app. If you perform a factory reset inside the app (System tab → Factory Reset), your cloud data is deleted immediately.

6. Children's Privacy

MobLife is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data has been stored, contact us at mobflow.dev@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

You can delete all of your data at any time by using the factory reset feature inside the app (System tab → Factory Reset). This deletes both local storage and your Firebase cloud document. You can also ask us to delete it without the app. See Delete Your Account and Data below.

8. Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your MobLife account and all data linked to it in either of these ways. You do not need to reinstall the app.

  1. In the app: open the System tab and tap Factory Reset. This deletes the data on your device and your cloud copy right away.
  2. Without the app: email mobflow.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my MobLife data." Include your sync ID if you have one. You can find it in the app's System tab, under Cloud Sync. We will delete your cloud data and anonymous account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted: your anonymous user ID, your habit logs, and your game state (XP, level, Mobs, quests, and boss progress). We do not keep a copy after deletion.
