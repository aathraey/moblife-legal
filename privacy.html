Privacy Policy

App: MobLife  |  Last updated: October 20, 2026  |  Contact: mobflow.dev@gmail.com

MobLife ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what your rights are.

1. Who We Are

MobLife is an independent app developed by Aathraey. Contact: mobflow.dev@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

None. Your habit logs and game progress (XP, level, Mobs, quests, boss progress) are stored only on your phone. The app does not send them anywhere.

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, payment information, or any health data.

3. Older Versions and Cloud Copies

Versions up to 2.0.0 also saved a copy of your progress in Firebase (Google Cloud), linked to a random anonymous ID with no name or email. When you update, the app deletes that copy and the anonymous ID once, then never contacts Firebase again. If you are offline, it tries again the next time you open the app.

5. Backups You Make

System → Export creates a backup file and opens your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes is your choice, and we never see it.

6. Children's Privacy

MobLife is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data has been stored, contact us at mobflow.dev@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

You can delete all of your data at any time with the factory reset inside the app (System tab → Factory Reset), or by uninstalling the app. See Delete Your Account and Data below.

8. Delete Your Account and Data

  1. In the app: open the System tab and tap Factory Reset. This deletes everything on your phone, and any cloud copy an older version left, right away.
  2. Without the app: uninstalling deletes everything on your phone. If you used an older version and want its cloud copy gone without updating, email mobflow.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my MobLife data." Include your old sync ID if you saved it. We will delete the matching cloud data and anonymous ID within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted: your habit logs and game state (XP, level, Mobs, quests, and boss progress), plus the anonymous ID if an older version created one. We do not keep a copy after deletion.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
